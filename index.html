An election you can follow from sign-up to result

Civic Ballot replaces paper slips and shared spreadsheets with one portal. Administrators approve who may take part, voters verify their identity by email, and everyone can watch the tally update as ballots arrive.

Whether it is a student council, a club or a society, the same simple flow applies: register, verify, vote, confirm.

🛡️

Approved voters only

An admin whitelists each email address. Nobody else can register.

✉️

Email verification

A 6-digit code proves you own the address before your account exists.

🗳️

One person, one vote

Each account holds a single ballot. Change your mind while polls are open and recast.

🔐

Receipts and audit log

Your receipt is a SHA-256 fingerprint of your ballot. Admin actions go into a hash-chained log.
